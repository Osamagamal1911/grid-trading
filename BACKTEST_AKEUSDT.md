## Methodology notes

- Same strategy code as live (local fills + mark price; no `ccxt` in logic).
- Simulated fills: limit/stop orders match on candle low/high range crossing (wicks trigger);
  stop-market fills at stop ± slippage (or at a gapping open beyond the trigger);
  stop-limit gap-misses survive (honest).
- Stop-outs do NOT halt the run: each models an operator redeploy (documented restart model).
- Sanity gates (throw on violation): stops never fire EARLY (at/above −stopLossPct required);
  crossed-but-unfilled resting stops throw (sim mismatch tripwire); every TP cycle net > 0.
- Liquidations table (wick ruin past −100% ROI):
  none.
- Open position at end-of-data (marked to close, excluded from win rate): long 8800 @ avg 0.031002000000000002 (-11.99% unrealized).
